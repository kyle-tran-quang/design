Gold: `window-row` (one height, one gap, the middle one is the story and the only one with words, neighbours are
filler that the crop may cut). Changed: phones (kit `Phone`, pro frame, dark) instead of windows; the phones run off
the top so their docks show; the look is Breadslice's own (colours sampled from the case study's hero image, Nunito,

crops its sides, with a fade at each side.
