(KYLE'S HONEST SELF)

Me as a person

Everyday I overthink about how people experiences a product, push through chaos, until I see something that finally works.

And here I am (still a bit tired at the end of the day) but I'm happy 'cause I enjoy it.

I cut the fluff and focus on what truly matters to users, getting insights early and often. But I never get satisfied so I always iterate.

I love connecting and resonating with others to know exactly how they feel about something, and giving them what wows in return.

Understanding how people think and feel through psychology helps me craft experiences "that won't annoy you".

And of course, I love putting playfulness into design to make experiences more engaging, memorable, and delightful :)
